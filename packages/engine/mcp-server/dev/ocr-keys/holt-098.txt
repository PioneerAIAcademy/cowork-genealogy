In Aug., 1828, in company with his son-in-law, Captain A. Wood, he removed to Carroll county, Ia., and purchased some property, and in 1820 took his family thither. Col. Holt died as he had lived, an honest, honorable and upright man. He was faithful to his country and fraternal to his fellow man. He d. at Delphi May 6, 1860, aged 90y. 8m. 11y.

1013. 7. ERASTUS⁶ (2073) b. Hampton Sep. 8, 1778; m. June 1, 1800, Sally, daughter of Isaiah Parmenter; removed in 1799 to the town of Stockbridge, Vt., where he m.; in 1803 removed to Pittsfield, Vt., at which place he was living 1861 in his 83d year. He has been justice of the peace 34 consecutive years; representative to general assembly 9 years consecutively, and 13 years in all; delegate to the constitutional convention 2 years; farmer, and attorney and counsellor. 12 ch.
