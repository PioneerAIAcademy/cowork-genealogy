299. 6. HANNAH⁵ b. Andover Dec. 19, 1745; m. Nathan Ballard; lived at Wilton and Concord, N. H. He d. June 14, 1835, aged 90.
Children of JACOB⁴ (87) and Mary (Osgood) Holt, 1st wife.
300. 1. JACOB⁵ (779) b. Andover Mar. 29, 1739; m. Mar. 22, 1764, Rhoda, dau. of Ephraim Abbot. She was b. July 3, 1741, and d. Jan., 1820, aged 79. He d. May 12, 1816, aged 77.
In the earlier part of his life he resided in the South Parish of Andover. He actively espoused the cause of his country at the beginning of the revolutionary war. He was at the battle of Bunker Hill, and was a brave and intrepid soldier. In 1795 he removed to the district of Maine and located himself in a township incorporated under the name of Albany, in the county of Oxford. This journey of 200 miles, over a rough and dreary road, was performed in 15 days with an ox sled covered with canvass; the sled containing furniture and other necessary articles, with the family, consisting of the father, mother, two sons and a daughter completing the lading of the vehicle. Two sons and a daughter had the year before cleared a few acres and built a log house and a barn. This was the 3d family that settled in that township. He was one of the number that organized the first church in Albany, and contributed largely for the support of schools, before any public provision had been made. 12 ch.
