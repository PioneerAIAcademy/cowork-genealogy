This accident slightly maimed him for life, which, together with the fact of his manifesting unusual aptitude and taste for study, induced his father to give him a liberal education. He was graduated at Harvard College 1790; studied divinity with Rev. Mr. French of Andover; ordained minister at Epping, N. H., Feb. 27, 1793. (Expenses of ordination £16, 15s., 9½d., on a salary of £90.) Removed to Exeter, N. H., 1821. Installed as pastor of Presbyterian Church, Peterborough, Mar. 7, 1827; resigned Apr., 1835. Preached in Deering, N. H., as stated supply, from 1835 to 1841. At the age of 80 he retired from the ministry to Greenfield, N. H., where he spent the remainder of his days. 7 ch.
486. 7. JOHN⁵ (1201) b. Andover Jan. 12, 1765; m. Jan., 1792, Dorcas, daughter of George Abbot. She was b. Dec. 18, 1766, and d. Mar. 15, 1841. He removed to Greenfield, N. H., and was deacon in the church. He was lieutenant of militia, and was called into service in the Shays rebellion. 8 ch.
487. 8. TIMOTHY⁵ (1209) b. Andover Apr. 1, 1767; m. Nov. 7, 1793, Lydia Holt. Removed to Peterborough, N. H., and lived on the land which he first improved. He was a deacon in the church; d. 1856, aged 89. 8 ch.
488. 9. SOLOMON⁵ (1217) b. Andover Dec. 15, 1768; m. May 22, 1798, Mary, daughter of Justin L. and Mary Cummings, who d. Oct. 8, 1852, aged 77. He was a deacon in the West Parish Church, Andover; was captain of militia, selectman, etc. He d. Apr. 15, 1830, aged 62. 8 ch.
489. 10. HANNAH⁵ b. Andover June 7, 1771; m. Nov. 27, 1794, Ephraim Holt of Greenfield, N. H. He was b. Mar. 19, 1769, and d. Oct. 24, 1836. She d. Apr. 21, 1842. See Ephraim Holt.
490. 11. STEPHEN⁵ (1225) b. Andover Apr. 28, 1773; m. 1799, Fanny Bowers. Removed to Greenfield, N. H., where he d. Apr., 1828, aged 53. He was a school teacher, deacon in the church, captain of militia, justice of the peace, etc. 8 ch.
491. 12. CHLOE⁵ b. Andover May 27, 1775; m. Oct. 23, 1798, Francis Bowers. She d. Oct., 1835, aged 60.
