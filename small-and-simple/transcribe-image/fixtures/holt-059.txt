HOLT FAMILY.
495. 2. HANNAH⁵ b. Andover Feb. 11, 1739; m. Nov. 29, 1759, Daniel Holt, her cousin. He was b. Feb. 10, 1733, and d. 1778, aged 45. She d. Aug. 2, 1831, aged 92.
496. 3. DANIEL⁵ (1235) b. Andover Sep. 11, 1740; m. Oct. 3, 1761, Alice, daughter of Benjamin Holt. She was b. Nov. 13, 1742. Lived at Lunenburg, Mass. 6 ch.
497. 4. ASA⁵ (1241) b. Andover May 3, 1743; m. 1st, Dinah, daughter of Samuel and Jemima (Gray) Holt. She was b. Nov. 6, 1744; m. 2d, July 5, 1781, Mrs. Lydia Patten, daughter of Jonathan and Jemima (Russel) Stevens. He d. Feb., 1793, aged 51. Lived at Wilmington, Mass.
498. 5. MEHITABLE⁵ b. Andover Feb. 8, 1744; m. May 28, 1761, Samuel Lafkin, and 2d, Abner Wilkins.
499. 6. ABIEL⁵ (1245) b. Andover Apr. 3, 1746; m. June 28, 1767, Lydia, daughter of Joshua Lovejoy. He d. Nov. 17, 1824, aged 78. 7 ch.
Children by Dorcas Holt, 2d wife.
500. 7. THOMAS⁵ b. Andover June 15, 1750; m. Oct. 27, 1774, Lydia Farnum, and removed to Amherst, N. H., 1783.
501. 8. DORCAS⁵ b. Andover Mar. 19, 1753.
502. 9. MARY⁵ b. Andover Mar. 11, 1578; m. May 21, 1776, Lieutenant John Adams.
503. 10. LOIS⁵ b. Andover Oct. 29, 1760; m. Jan. 4, 1785, Moses Pearson of Wilmington, Mass.
504. 11. WILLIAM⁵ (1252) b. Andover Sep. 7, 1763; m. July 29, 1784, Elizabeth, daughter of Jacob Jones. She d. at Weld, Me., 1829. He removed to Wilton, N. H., subsequently returned to Andover, and probably d. Dec. 23, 1810. 8 ch.
505. 12. JOSEPH⁵ (1260) b. Andover Sep. 29, 1766; m. Nov. 27, 1788, Abigail, daughter of Samuel and Abigail Holt. She d. May 13, 1821, aged 54. He d. June 7, 1791. Lived at Andover. 2 ch.
Children of JOSEPH⁴ (150) and Mary (Abbot) Holt.
506. 1. JOSEPH⁵ b. Lunenburg Apr. 8, 1744; d. July 26, 1744.
507. 2. MARY⁵ b. Lunenburg, Aug. 17, 1745; m. June 26, 1776, Benjamin Darling.
508. 3. SARAH⁵ b. Lunenburg bapt. Dec. 17, 1746.
509. 4. ABIEL⁵ (1262) b. Lunenburg July 18, 1748; m. 1775, Sarah, daughter of Job Abbot. She was b. 1751, and d. Oct. 9, 1854, aged 103.
