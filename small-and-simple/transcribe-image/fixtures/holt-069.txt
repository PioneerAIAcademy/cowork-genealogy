HOLT FAMILY.
Children of SAMUEL⁵ (219) and Abigail (Blanchard) Holt.
654. 1. SAMUEL⁶ (1521) b. Andover Sep. 7, 1761; m. Sep. 8, 1785, Sarah Massey; removed to Watertown, Mass., where he d. She d. Mar. 6, 1813. He was a soldier in the revolutionary war. 4 ch.
655. 2. JONATHAN⁶ b. Andover 1762; d. Feb. 9, 1825.
656. 3. ISAAC⁶ (1525) b. Andover Jan. 21, 1764; m. Dec. 6, 1789, Tabitha Blunt, who d. June 24, 1840, aged 75. He d. July 25, 1843, aged 80. 11 ch.
657. 4. ABIGAIL⁶ b. Andover May 19 1767; m. Nov. 27, 1788, Joseph Holt. He was b. Sep. 29, 1766, and d. June 7, 1791, aged 24y, 8m. She d. May 13, 1821, aged 54.
658. 5. ELIZABETH⁶ b. Andover May 19, 1767; twin; m. at Boston, Mass., Nov. 5, 1799, Jared Carroll. He was b. Walpole, N. H., Feb. 28, 1770, and d. in Greenwich, Miss., July 13, 1819. She d. May 5, 1825.
659. 6. PERSIS⁶ b. Andover Nov. 21, 1775; d. young.
Children of DAVID⁵ (222) and Hannah (Martin) Holt.
660. 1. DARIUS⁶ (1535) b. Andover Mar. 6, 1763; m. May 24, 1785, Chloe, daughter of Abiel Holt. She d. at Norway, Me., Oct. 11, 1849, aged 80. He d. Aug., 1854, aged 91. 14 ch.
He was a soldier in the revolutionary war, and was with General Wayne when he stormed and took Stony Point. During his service as sentinel he had his skull fractured, but it was trepanned successfully, and, by the insertion of a piece of silver into the wound, he recovered. The silver he wore in his head to the day of his death. He had, in 1854, two sisters and a brother living at Andover, whose united ages was 243 years. One of these sisters, Hannah, then the oldest inhabitant of the town, three-quarters of a century ago, fried doughnuts to supply the knapsacks of her youthful and patriotic brother at his departure for the seat of war. After the war, 1789, he removed to Norway, Me., where he d. 14 ch.
661. 2. HANNAH⁶ b. Andover Sep. 13, 1765; d. May 12, 1856, aged 90; unmarried.
662. 3. MARY⁶ (1549) b. Andover Feb. 4, 1767; m. June 6, 1793, Samuel Hunt of Cambridge, Mass., as her 1st husband. He was b. Sep. 17, 1763. 6 ch.
