251. 8. SAMUEL⁵ (711) b. Andover May 1, 1749; m. 1770, Lydia, dau. of Ephraim Adams of Dunstable, Mass. She was b. July 27, 1749, and d. June 24, 1844, aged 94, 11. He d. at Temple, N. Baldwin's Regt., and served till Dec. ensuing. 10 ch.
