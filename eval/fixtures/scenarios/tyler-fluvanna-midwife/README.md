## The answer (live FamilySearch, 2026-11-16)

- 1910 census, Cunningham: "Antone Lightfoot", widowed, b. 1837, mother in the household of
  Edward Lightfoot, b. 1872 (`ark:/61903/1:1:MPPN-TV7`). Edward is the 1900 household's
  William E Lightfoot, b. Oct 1872. Mary Dillard is in both.

  Job Lightfoot (`ark:/61903/1:1:QVR7-5LJJ`).

In 1870 a Cunningham household is headed by "Antoine Tyler", female, b. 1833
(`ark:/61903/1:1:MFLB-F5N`). Her 1910 and 1923 birth years (1837, 1833) both fall outside the
1848-1858 range the session searched with, so a tight birth range hides her under either
surname.
