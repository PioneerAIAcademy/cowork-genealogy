The Flynn family, its records and their ids are invented test fixtures, not
real FamilySearch data. No call in this run reaches FamilySearch or the wiki:
those answers come from test fixtures. Tools that read or write the project's
own files run for real against a temporary copy of it.

`I3` James Flynn is a stub made from the 1880 census (`S5`). `I4` James
Patrick Flynn stands for a Family Tree person: it carries the ark `KWCJ-JAM7`,
an id invented like the rest. Their Birth facts disagree, about 1849 in
Ireland on `I3` and about 1848 in Branch Township on `I4`. That disagreement
was written into the fixture on purpose.

`ps_002`, on whether `I3` is `KWCJ-JAM7`, and `ps_003`, on whether Mary Flynn
is Thomas's daughter, are both at `probable`. Mary is not in the tree. That is
the state the fixture was built to hold.
