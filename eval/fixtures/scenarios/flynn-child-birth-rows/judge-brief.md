Margaret Flynn (I3), daughter of Patrick Flynn (I1), was born about 1897 in Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania; q_003 asks when and where.

The project's locality entry loc_001 carries `record_types` rows. Its vital_record row says the county orphans' courts kept registers of births and deaths for 1893-1905, and its court row repeats it, citing the FamilySearch Wiki's Pennsylvania Probate Records page. That row is the only place in the project that says so. Pennsylvania began state birth certificates only in 1906, so a birth about 1897 is recorded civilly only in that county register, and otherwise in church baptism records.
