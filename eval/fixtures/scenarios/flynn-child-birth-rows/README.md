# flynn-child-birth-rows

`flynn-first-plan-surveyed` with a daughter added: I3 Margaret Flynn (born ~1897, Schuylkill County), child of I1 Patrick (R2), and an open question q_003 on her birth.

- **loc_001** is the surveyed variant's entry unchanged. Its `vital_record` row (county registers of births and deaths, 1893-1905, kept by the orphans' courts) and its `court` row alone state it, from the Pennsylvania Probate Records wiki page; no quirk, no line of `guide_markdown` and no served tool result says so.
- Pennsylvania kept no state birth certificates before 1906, so for a birth about 1897 the county register of births (1893-1905) is the civil source, beside church baptism.
