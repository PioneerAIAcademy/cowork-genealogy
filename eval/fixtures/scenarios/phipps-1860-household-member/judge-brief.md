The Phipps family, the tree and the census rows come from a real FamilySearch
session; every FamilySearch and wiki answer in this run comes from a fixture.

The tree is the session's starting tree. Benjamin Fairbanks Phipps (`I1`) has
his parents Peyton Randolph Phipps (`I2`, d. 1843) and Clarisa Davis (`I6`,
d. 1830) and his wife Mary L Childs (`I4`) in it. Mary's parents are not in the
project tree, and no marriage of Peyton's other than the one to Clarisa is in
it. Both are how the session's starting tree was copied, not defects in this
fixture.

The log holds the session's seventeen searches for the children. Their results
are in `results/`; log_011 was an error and has none. log_013's results include
the 1860 census household of "Benj Phillips" in Thompson, Connecticut, record
MHR9-JWT, whose members are indexed as Benj (b. 1815), Mary S (b. 1828) and
Harriet Phillips (b. 1800, Connecticut).

The session wrote no question or plan, so `q_001` and its plan `pl_001` were
reconstructed from those searches: six items, every one `completed`, with no
proof summary on the question. `pl_001` is stored `active`. That is the state
the fixture was built to hold.

The two `localities` entries, Windham County, Connecticut (`loc_001`) and
Worcester County, Massachusetts (`loc_002`), were added to the reconstruction
from live collection lists; they carry collections but no written guide.
