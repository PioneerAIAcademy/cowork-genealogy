# england-census-hypothesis

A non-US hypothesis scenario: two tree persons, every fact in England. Built from
`england-census-timeline` for a Mode B (hypothesis) timeline test.

**Persons:** William Ashford I1 (birth about 1838 at Canterbury, Kent, England; the 1851
census at Canterbury) and William Ashford I2 (marriage at Canterbury 1859; death there 1874).
All persons are deceased.

**Hypothesis `h_001` (active):** I2 is the same person as I1. The four records fit one life
in one town, so a candidate timeline built across both persons coheres.

Only the 1851 census is documented. Across that one life he is alive and resident in England
for the 1841, 1861 and 1871 English decennial censuses with no record of them, and none of
those is a US federal census year. A hypothesis timeline reads census years from the
`England_Census` wiki page (Mode A takes them from `timeline_build`'s census table instead),
so the expected years show whether that page was read.
