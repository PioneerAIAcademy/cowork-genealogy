The Phipps family, the tree and the census records come from a real
FamilySearch session; every FamilySearch answer in this run comes from a
fixture or from the project files.

The project holds one question, `q_001`, asking for all the children of
Benjamin Fairbanks Phipps (`I1`), with no plan yet. The 1850, 1860, 1870 and
1880 census records of his household are extracted into `sources` and
`assertions` and linked to tree persons in `person_evidence`. A woman named
Harriet is indexed in the 1850 (Harriet G Phipps, born about 1800,
Massachusetts), 1860 (Harriet Phillips, born about 1800, Connecticut) and 1870
(Harriet Phipps) households; no tree person is named Harriet.

The project's other records and searches were trimmed out of this fixture to
keep it small. Their absence is the fixture's construction, not a defect in
the project. Two `localities` entries (Windham County, Connecticut and
Worcester County, Massachusetts) were added to it.
