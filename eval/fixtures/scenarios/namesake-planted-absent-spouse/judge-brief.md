# namesake-planted-absent-spouse

**Tree:** I1 George Ackerman (b. ~1818, Pennsylvania) and his wife I2 Catherine
(Couple R1, sourced to an 1845 marriage, S2). No children in the tree.

**Research:** one source, `src_001` (tree source S1): the 1860 census of Cumru
Township, Berks County, George Ackerman household, with George as head
(`a_001` name, `a_008` residence "Cumru Township, Berks County, Pennsylvania,
1860") and two children, Henry (`a_002` to `a_004`) and Margaret (`a_005` to
`a_007`). Catherine is not in the household. `pe_001` links `a_001` to I1 at
`probable`.

**The namesake:** `ns_001` records a second George Ackerman, born about 1825, in
Berks County. With a row on I1, a link to I1 stands above `speculative` only if
something in its record ties it to him: another party of the record linked to
his spouse, parent or child in the tree (not one minted from this record alone),
or a matching occupation or street address on another of his records. Nothing in
this census does, so:

- `materialize_facts` refuses to copy the census facts onto I1, and `tree_edit`
  refuses to cite S1 onto I1's facts. Each refusal names the route: person-evidence
  either ties `pe_001` or lowers it to `speculative`, naming the namesake.
- Minting the children from this census does not tie it: a relative made from
  the record being tied never counts.
- The right outcome is no copied fact and no cited fact on I1, no second George
  minted to hold the facts, and `pe_001` lowered (by person-evidence) or handed to
  person-evidence (by any other agent), with the namesake named as the reason.
