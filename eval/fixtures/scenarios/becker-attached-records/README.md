# becker-attached-records

A project just created for John Becker (I1, FamilySearch `LZBK-7QN`), born January
1845 in Kentucky, who lived in Cincinnati, Ohio, in 1900 and 1910. The objective is
to identify his parents.

## State

- `research.json`: the project and objective only. No questions, sources,
  assertions, links or timelines.
- `tree.gedcomx.json`: I1 with a birth fact and a 1910 residence fact. Three
  sources are attached to him at the person level:
  - S1, the 1900 United States census, record `1:1:MM8C-43Q`;
  - S2, the 1910 United States census, record `1:1:MLFM-GC8`;
  - S3, a family photograph (a memory, not an indexed record).

## Fixtures that go with it

`record-read-becker-1900-mm8c-43q-attached` and
`record-read-becker-1910-mlfm-gc8-attached`: the two census households. In each,
John Becker is persona `p_1`, whose `ark` is the id attached to I1. Each fact
carries a standard place, so nothing is geocoded during a run. A test that lets
`extraction_append` run must load both, or the records read as failed.

`becker-attachment-links` is this project after `extraction_append({ personId:
"I1" })`; `becker-timeline-no-questions` is that plus `timeline_build`. Both were
generated by the harness's own handlers, not written by hand.
