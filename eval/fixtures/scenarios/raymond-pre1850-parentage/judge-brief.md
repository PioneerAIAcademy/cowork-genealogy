# raymond-pre1850-parentage

Synthetic scenario: the two Paul Raymonds are not real people, and every tool response is mocked from a fixture.

This is a first plan. The plans, log, sources, assertions and every other research section are empty by design, and the one question is in progress with no plan behind it yet.

The tree holds the two men linked as father and son, with no sources. That is intentional, not missing data. The son's birthplace is blank on purpose.

The two localities were written for this scenario and describe Addison County, Vermont, and Berkshire County, Massachusetts. Their collection lists and quirks are deliberate.
