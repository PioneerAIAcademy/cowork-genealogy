# mid-research-flynn-namesakes

A copy of `mid-research-flynn` (same tree, same research) with two searches added for the
namesake tests of search-records. The copy keeps the shared scenario unchanged, since every
suite that references a scenario hashes it into its run-log snapshot.

- **Added to `pl_002`** (q_001, Patrick's parents): `pli_007`, a planned search of Irish
  Catholic baptisms, 1843-1847, whose rationale says it may name his parents.
- **Added question `q_003`** (whom Patrick married, open) with **plan `pl_003`** (active) and
  its one item `pli_008`, a planned search of Schuylkill County Catholic marriages, 1865-1880,
  whose rationale says it may name his wife.

Everything else is `mid-research-flynn` as it stands: Patrick Flynn (I1, born about 1845 in
Ireland, died 12 March 1908 in Schuylkill County) with one parent in the tree, Thomas Flynn
(I2); and the links pe_004 and pe_005 on I1 stand with nothing in their records tying them
to him, so a namesakes row on I1 draws research_append's warning about both.
