# person-less-project (judge brief)

A project made by `project_create` with no `personId`: the objective is the researcher's request ("Research request with no person in it"), `subject_person_ids` is empty and the tree holds no persons, relationships or sources.

Every other section of `research.json` is empty: no questions, plans, log entries, sources or assertions. A `questions` append is refused here with a message saying research on a person starts a new project from a FamilySearch person ID; log entries, sources and localities write normally.
