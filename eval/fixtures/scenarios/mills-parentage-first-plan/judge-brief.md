# mills-parentage-first-plan (judge brief)

Real research, carved from a beta session at the point the first research plan was requested.
FamilySearch, wiki and place answers come from test fixtures; the tools that read or write the
project's own files run for real against a copy of it.

The tree came from the FamilySearch Family Tree at project creation. Its facts and
relationships are the tree's claims; none has been checked against a record in this project
yet. That includes the parent links from I1 Alexander Mills to I5 George Mills and I7 Abigail
Weed, and the two marriage dates on R1 and R2.

There is no `localities` entry for any Massachusetts or New Hampshire place. That is how the
project stood at the time, not a fixture defect.
