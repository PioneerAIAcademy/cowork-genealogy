Iram F. Hyde and his family are real, deceased Family Tree persons. The project
comes from a recorded research session, migrated to the current schema; its
records, ids and log notes are as that session found them.

The project has two questions. q_001 (Iram's birth state) is resolved, with
proof summary `ps_001` at probable. q_002 (his parents) is in progress, with
proof summary `ps_002` at not_proved. Conflict `c_001` records the birthplace
disagreement between the federal censuses (New Hampshire) and the 1855 New York
state census (Washington County, New York).

The research log runs from `log_001` to `log_021`, in the order the searches
were made. Each entry's `notes` field says what was found. Search results for
the indexed searches are stored beside `research.json` as `results/log_NNN.json`;
`log_019`, `log_020` and `log_021` were image and full-text reads with no
results file, so their findings are in their `notes` only.

`hypotheses` is empty, and no parent of Iram is in the tree.
