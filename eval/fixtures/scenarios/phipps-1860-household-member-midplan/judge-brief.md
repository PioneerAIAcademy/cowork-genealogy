The Phipps family, the tree and the census rows come from a real FamilySearch
session; every FamilySearch and wiki answer in this run comes from a fixture.

The tree is the session's starting tree. Benjamin Fairbanks Phipps (`I1`) has
his parents Peyton Randolph Phipps (`I2`, d. 1843) and Clarisa Davis (`I6`,
d. 1830) and his wife Mary L Childs (`I4`) in it. Mary's parents are not in the
project tree, and no marriage of Peyton's other than the one to Clarisa is in
it. Both are how the session's starting tree was copied.

The question `q_001` asks for Benjamin's children. Its plan `pl_001` is
`active`: `pli_001`, the 1860 Thompson census, is `completed` and owns log_013
and log_014; `pli_002`, the children's vital records 1849-1878, is still
`planned`. log_013's results include the 1860 household of "Benj Phillips",
record MHR9-JWT, whose members are indexed as Benj (b. 1815), Mary S (b. 1828)
and Harriet Phillips (b. 1800, Connecticut).

This scenario was cut down from a larger one to keep it small; the project's
other searches and their plan items are not in it. That is the fixture's
construction, not a defect in the project.
