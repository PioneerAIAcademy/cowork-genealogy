# mayfield-disputed-given-name

Synthetic scenario: the Mayfield family is not real, and every FamilySearch and wiki answer comes from a test fixture. The tools that read or write the project's own files run for real against a copy of it.

This is a first plan. The plans, person_evidence, conflicts, hypotheses, timelines and proof_summaries sections are empty by design, and the one question is in progress with no plan behind it yet.

The question's subject has no given name in the tree, and her death is not recorded anywhere in the project. Both are intentional, not missing data.

The given-name readings in the assertions disagree with one another on purpose.

The locality was written for this scenario. Its short collection list and its quirk are deliberate.
