FamilySearch tree; every tool response in the test is mocked. One record is
synthetic: `src_003`, the 1869 San Francisco city directory entry, with its

plays no part in this test. The other Gaetjen men named in the log notes
(`log_005`, `log_006`, `log_019`) are search history.
