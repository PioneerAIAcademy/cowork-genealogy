# Locality Guide: Kľak (Klak), Bars County, Kingdom of Hungary (1844)

## Jurisdiction overview
- **Historical name:** Klak, Bars County, Kingdom of Hungary
- **Modern name:** Kľak, Žarnovica District, Slovakia
- **Administrative center:** Nová Baňa (nearest administrative town)
- **Parent jurisdiction:** Bars County (Tekov), Kingdom of Hungary → Austro-Hungarian Empire
- **Population during period:** No indexed figures returned; small rural village
- **Economy:** Agriculture; mining region (Nová Baňa area was a historic gold/silver mining district)
- **Ethnic composition:** Predominantly Slovak-speaking population in a Hungarian-administered county
- **Religious denominations:** Roman Catholic (dominant); minority Lutheran presence possible
- **Key historical events:** 1844 falls under Kingdom of Hungary within the Austrian Empire; Hungarian civil registration did not begin until 1 October 1895 — all vital records for 1844 are exclusively church records

## Boundary changes
- In 1844, Klak was in **Bars County (Bars vármegye)**, Kingdom of Hungary, Austrian Empire
- After WWI (1918), the territory became part of Czechoslovakia; territory designated "Tekov"
- Since 1993, part of independent Slovakia — now Žarnovica District, Banská Bystrica Region
- Records filed under Bars/Klak (Hungarian) in historical catalogs correspond to Kľak/Žarnovica (Slovak) in modern finding aids
