## Locality Guide: Sindlingen, Hesse-Nassau — circa 1870

### Jurisdictional Context

Sindlingen is a small village on the Main River west of Frankfurt. In 1870 it lay in the former Duchy of Nassau territory, which Prussia had annexed in 1866 and incorporated into the Province of Hesse-Nassau (Hessen-Nassau). FamilySearch records it under two successive standard places: **"Sindlingen, Frankfurt, Duchy of Nassau"** (before 1866) and **"Sindlingen, Höchst, Hesse-Nassau, Prussia, Germany"** (from 1886 forward). Records created in the 1866–1886 transition window—including an 1870 birth—are indexed under both designations in different tools. Sindlingen was a Catholic parish in the **Diocese of Limburg (Bistum Limburg)**, organized in 1827 from the former Dioceses of Trier and Mainz, covering the Duchy of Nassau, Frankfurt, and surrounding areas.
