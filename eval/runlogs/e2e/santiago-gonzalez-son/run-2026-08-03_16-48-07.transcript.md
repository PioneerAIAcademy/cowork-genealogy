# Locality Guide: Guayaquil, Guayas, Ecuador (1900–1940)

## Jurisdiction overview
- **Founded:** City established July 25, 1538; Guayaquil Canton formally delineated 1824 through the Law of Territorial Division of the Republic of Colombia
- **Administrative center:** Guayaquil (capital of Guayas Province)
- **Parent jurisdiction:** Guayas Province → Ecuador
- **Population during period:** Canton population approximately 3,051,000 (modern figure; early 20th-century city was ~80,000–100,000)
- **Economy:** Dominant port city of Ecuador; cacao export hub, commerce and trade
- **Ethnic composition:** Mixed mestizo majority; Afro-Ecuadorian communities; small foreign merchant colonies (British, German, Italian)
- **Religious denominations:** Predominantly Roman Catholic; Protestant minority
- **Key historical events:** Bubonic plague epidemic 1908; rapid growth as cacao export center 1900–1920; economic depression after cacao blight 1920s

## Boundary changes
- Stable canton boundaries throughout 1900–1940 period
- Province of Santa Elena was separated from Guayas in 2007 — no effect on this research period
- Records for this period fall entirely within Guayaquil Canton, Guayas Province

## Available record types

### Vital records (civil registration)
- **Start date:** Civil registration began nationally in 1900
- **What exists:** Births, marriages, and deaths registered at the municipal civil registration office in Guayaquil
- **Where held:** Original records at the local Registro Civil office; copies at the **Dirección General del Registro Civil**, Av. Amazonas N37-61 y Naciones Unidas, Quito (Guayas provincial office: 043-802-300 ext. 3200; website: registrocivil.gob.ec)
- **Online status — DEATH records:** FamilySearch Catalog holds "Defunciones Registros civiles, 1862–1928" (catalog ID 205794), browse-only images. The `volume_search` returned 69 death-index volumes for Guayaquil, many partially or fully indexed (60–100% name-searchable), covering 1901–1940.
- **Online status — BIRTH records:** ⚠️ **No birth or baptism volumes for Guayaquil appeared in the FamilySearch volume search (69 volumes returned, all death indexes).** The FamilySearch Getting Started wiki explicitly warns: *"Civil registration was officially instituted in 1900. These records are typically not available online."* Civil birth registers from 1900–onward are **not digitized on FamilySearch**; researchers must contact the Registro Civil directly.
- **Information in birth records:** Child's name, date and place of birth, parents' names, ages, marital status, nationality, residence, father's occupation, names of witnesses

### Church records
- **Denomination:** Roman Catholic (overwhelmingly dominant in Guayaquil)
- **Record types:** Baptisms, marriages, burials, confirmations
- **Online access:** **Ecuador, Catholic Church Records, 1565–2023** (FamilySearch collection 1923402) — **indexed + images**, 3,867,977 records, 23,571,866 persons. This is the primary online path for church baptisms ca. 1915.
- **Also available:** **Ecuador, Baptisms, 1680–1930** (collection 1520610) — index only, 399,934 records. Also mirrored on Ancestry and MyHeritage ($).
- **⚠️ Coverage caveat:** The FamilySearch Getting Started wiki states: *"Church records for the cities of Quito and Guayaquil are typically not available online."* Coverage of Guayaquil parish registers in these collections may be incomplete; results must be verified against the images.
- **Language:** Spanish (all records)
- **Physical records:** Parish archives and Diocese of Guayaquil archives hold original registers not in the online collections

### Census records
- **Available:** Ecuador, Census, 1737–1990 (collection 5000097) — **indexed + images**, 990,742 records. Covers a wide date range but actual 20th-century Guayaquil census coverage within this collection is not confirmed from tool results; search is warranted.
- **Pre-1900 censuses:** Departamento de Empadronamientos Padrones, 1825 (FamilySearch Catalog 400007) — browse-only images. Not relevant for 1915.
- **Access:** Free on FamilySearch

### Cemetery records
- **Available:** Ecuador, Cemetery Records, 1862–2020 (collection 3755446) — **indexed + images**, 992,024 records
- **Major cemeteries in Guayaquil:** Cementerio Patrimonial (main/general), Cementerio de los Extranjeros, Panteón Patrimonial de la Junta de Beneficencia, Cementerio Parque de la Paz "Pascuales," Cementerio Angel María Canals, Cementerio de Pascuales
- **FamilySearch Catalog supplement:** Ecuador, Guayas, Guayaquil, Cementerio Patrimonial, registros del cementerio, 1901–2015 (catalog 3165864)
- **Value:** Cemetery records can name parents/spouse and occasionally supply birth information useful for identifying siblings or confirming family units

### Marriages
- **Available:** Ecuador, Marriages, 1680–1930 (collection 1520614) — index only, 106,660 records. Also on Ancestry and MyHeritage ($). Also within Ecuador Catholic Church Records (1923402).

### Probate and court records
- No probate or court record collections for Guayaquil were returned by FamilySearch tools for this period. These would be held at local civil courts and provincial archives in Guayaquil; not digitized online.

### Military records
- No FamilySearch collections for Ecuadorian military records in this period surfaced in tool results. Ecuador military records are listed as a record type on the wiki but no online collections are named. Physical records at national archives in Quito.

### Newspaper records
- Not returned by FamilySearch tools. Guayaquil had active newspapers (El Telégrafo, El Universo) in this period. Holdings at Biblioteca Municipal de Guayaquil and Biblioteca Nacional in Quito; not yet digitized online.

## Research tips
- **Start with Catholic Church Records (1923402)** — This is the largest indexed+images collection and the best online path for a baptism ca. 1915. Search first, then verify images.
- **Ecuador Baptisms (1520610) is index-only** — If a hit appears, the original image is not attached; must locate the underlying register in the catalog or request it.

- **Spanish naming customs:** In Ecuador, children typically carry the father's first surname followed by the mother's first surname. The son would likely appear as "Manuel de Jesús González Tumbaco" in records — search under both "González" and "Gonzalez" (with and without accent).
- **Sociedad Amigos de la Genealogía (SAG)** based in Ecuador may hold compiled genealogies or indexes for Guayaquil families not yet on FamilySearch.
- **The Guayas provincial wiki notes no online church records for the province** — this reinforces the FamilySearch coverage gap for Guayaquil church registers.
