## Locality Guide: Wanroij, Noord-Brabant, Netherlands (ca. 1700–1720)

### Jurisdiction overview
- **Pre-1815 name:** Wanroij, Brabant, Netherlands (Generaliteitsland under the Dutch Republic)
- **Post-1815 name:** Wanroij, North Brabant, Netherlands (municipality within Noord-Brabant Province, formed 1815; merged 1993)
- **Parent jurisdiction (target period):** Brabant, Netherlands (Generaliteitsland; administered by the States-General, not an autonomous province)
- **Population:** No population data returned for the target period.
- **Religious denomination:** Predominantly **Roman Catholic**. Noord-Brabant remained almost entirely Catholic throughout the early modern period. Catholic DTB records are written in **Latin**.
- **Key historical context:** Wanroij fell in the Generaliteitsland — territory conquered from Spain during the Eighty Years' War and governed directly by the States-General of the Dutch Republic. It was not a recognized province, which affected administrative record-keeping. Civil registration did not exist; all vital record-keeping was through the Catholic parish.

### Boundary changes
- Pre-1815: falls under **Wanroij, Brabant, Netherlands** (Generaliteitsland jurisdiction)
- From 1815: falls under **Wanroij, North Brabant, Netherlands** (Noord-Brabant Province established under the Kingdom of the Netherlands)
- **For records in the target period (1700–1720):** search under the pre-1815 jurisdiction. The same physical registers continue across the boundary; treat them as one continuous record set despite the differing place names.

- **Start date:** 1 March 1811 (Napoleonic Civil Registration)

### Research tips
1. **Search indexed collections first** — try WieWasWie, BHIC, and FS collection 1778468 before committing to browse-only image reading.
2. **Browse-only warning** — the FamilySearch Noord-Brabant Church Records collection (2037960) has NO name index; browsing is image-by-image. Volume 005844878_001 (84 images, 1656–1723) is the highest-priority volume for our period.
3. **Latin records** — Catholic baptism entries use Latin. Key terms: *baptizatus/a* (baptized), *filius/filia* (son/daughter), *patrini* (godparents). Use the [Dutch Genealogical Word List](https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Dutch_Genealogical_Word_List) and the [Latin Genealogical Word List](https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Latin_Genealogical_Word_List).
4. **Naming customs** — Dutch Catholic families often reused names across generations and among siblings (a child who died young might have their name given to the next child). The name Elisabeth would be a significant family name.
5. **Name spelling variation** — surname "Jansen" may appear as Janssen, Jans, Jansz; "Hoffmans" as Hofmans, Hoffman, Hoffman. Search variants.
6. **Sibling search strategy** — the wiki recommends searching for all siblings when a baptism record is found; a negative sibling search (no Elisabeth) is as valuable as a positive hit.
7. **Search neighboring parishes** if not found in Wanroij — Westerbeek, Mill, and Sint Anthonis are nearby.
8. **Pre-1815 jurisdiction label** — FamilySearch indexes and volume waypoints may show the place as "Wanroij, North Brabant, Netherlands" even for pre-1815 volumes (the post-1815 name is applied retroactively). Searching both place name variants is advisable.
9. **Genealogie Werkbalk** (genealogiewerkbalk.nl/fs/) provides a user-friendly alternative browse interface for FS images.
