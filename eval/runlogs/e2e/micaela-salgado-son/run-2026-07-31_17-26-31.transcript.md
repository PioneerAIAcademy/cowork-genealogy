## Locality Guide: Tegucigalpa (Departamento), Honduras (circa 1889)

### Jurisdiction overview
- **Department formed:** 1838 from predecessor jurisdiction
- **Administrative center:** City of Tegucigalpa (capital of Honduras; seat of Distrito Central)
- **Parent jurisdiction:** Republic of Honduras
- **Population (Francisco Morazán dept):** ~59,000 (1881 est.); ~60,200 (1887 est.); ~77,700 (1901 est.) — Honduras national ~407,280 (1889 est.)
- **Economy:** Administrative/governmental capital; silver mining historically important; agriculture
- **Religious denominations:** Predominantly Roman Catholic; Catedral San Miguel Arcángel served as the main parish for the city of Tegucigalpa
- **Key historical events:** Honduras capital established in Tegucigalpa in 1880; the department was renamed Francisco Morazán in 1943

### Boundary changes
- 1542–1821: Province of Tegucigalpa under Captaincy General of Guatemala (New Spain)
- 1821–1838: Tegucigalpa Department under Federal Republic of Central America
- **1838–1943: "Tegucigalpa" Department, Republic of Honduras** ← period of interest (1889)
- 1943–present: Renamed **Francisco Morazán** Department
- **Researcher note:** FamilySearch volume data filed under "Francisco Morazán" reflects the modern name. When browsing, both department names refer to the same territory — records for 1889 will be physically labelled "Tegucigalpa" but indexed under "Francisco Morazán / Distrito Central."
